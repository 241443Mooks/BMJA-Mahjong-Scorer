## Source pin

Published source:

- Vincent Cheah and Tom Sloper, **Malaysian 3 Player Mah-Jongg**: https://www.sloperama.com/mahjongg/malaysian.html

The page states that Vincent Cheah supplied most of the material and Tom Sloper edited/presented it. The page copyright is 2003 and later Q&A material records subsequent reader clarifications and uncertainties.

The source itself says **table rules may vary** and later correspondence on the page demonstrates genuine local disagreement/variation. Therefore this A1 corpus is the **Cheah/Sloper published Malaysian 3P profile**, not “the rules of Malaysian Mahjong”.

## Profile facts

The published profile uses a three-player setup with an 84-tile active set:

- Dots only as the numbered suit;
- Winds and Dragons;
- Flowers/Seasons, Faces and Animals as bonus tiles;
- four `Fly` wild tiles;
- five-fan minimum to win;
- no normal round-wind cycle; winner becomes next dealer under the page's published procedure.

These facts are retained as profile/runtime evidence, not extra Encyclopaedia count rows unless separately represented below.

## Evidence cautions

This is deliberately a source-local profile rather than a universalisation:

- the page says table fan rules vary;
- later correspondence on the same page clarifies/contests the East-wind treatment;
- another Malaysian 164-tile tradition is separately described later on the page;
- a modern Mahjong Archive guide documents a different bounded Malaysian 3P interpretation sourced from another PDF/author.

These differences are evidence **for** keeping identities separate until 251A2, not reasons to average them together.

## 251A1 treatment

Count **34 Cheah/Sloper-Malaysian-3P-local records**.

Do not map Fly, bonus-tile, Seven Pairs, Nine Gates, wind/dragon, replacement-win or other names onto Singapore/Cantonese/MCR/Riichi concepts until exact predicates and profile identities can be compared in A2.
