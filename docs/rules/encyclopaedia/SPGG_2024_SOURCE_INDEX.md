## Source pin

First-party source: Singapore Polytechnic Graduates' Guild (SPGG), **Mahjong Rules & Regulations**, document footer `Version SPGG Mahjong Competition — January 2024`:

- https://www.spgg.org.sg/web/content/114907/

The source conclusion says the rules were adopted from NUS Mahjong Club and reviewed after SPGG's May 2023 competition to suit SPGG competition style. It also says SPGG competitions will follow the rules/version and explicitly acknowledges that rules may be changed for different groups.

Therefore this is a **dated named organiser competition profile**, not evidence that all Singapore Mahjong uses the same rules.
