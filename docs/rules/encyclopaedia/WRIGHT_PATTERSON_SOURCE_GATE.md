Status: **strong current authority pinned; paid/copyrighted catalogue not counted**

Date checked: 18 September 2026

## Current authority

First-party source: Wright-Patterson Spouses' Club (WPSC), Mah Jongg page:

- https://wrightpattersonosc.org/mah-jongg.html

The WPSC identifies itself as the home of the Wright-Patterson rules tradition and currently sells only the **2022 revised editions**.

The first-party sales page describes the 2022 Green Rules Book as containing **rules, hands and scoring tables** and explicitly says copyright restrictions apply and reproductions may not be made.

The current 2026 order form independently confirms the 2022 Green Rules Book as the current product and describes it as `Definitions, includes Rules, hands, scoring tables`.

## Publicly visible source-local evidence

The WPSC page publishes current corrections/clarifications for numbered 2022 hands, including references to hands #8, #9, #17, #18, #27, #28, #36, #37, #39, #41, #43, #48 and #69, plus rules clarifications such as no Jokers and concealed-hand treatment.

Those public corrections demonstrate a finite numbered hand catalogue but do **not** expose enough of the complete paid catalogue to reconstruct it safely or fairly.

## 251A1 treatment

Do **not** count or reconstruct the Wright-Patterson hand catalogue from secondary sites.

Admission gate:

1. obtain the intended current 2022 rules/hands material through legitimate access;
2. respect its copyright/licensing terms;
3. index only minimal factual metadata appropriate for the Encyclopaedia/source model rather than reproducing the proprietary hand descriptions or visual aid;
4. verify the full numbered catalogue and scoring values from the owned/authorised source;
5. only then decide whether it should become a counted source-local corpus.

This source is therefore in the same broad **access/rights-gated** class as the current NMJL annual target catalogue, while being a distinct Wright-Patterson rules tradition.

## Why record the gate now

Future research should not restart from generic Wright-Patterson search results or scrape third-party copies. The authority and current edition are already known; the remaining issue is legitimate source access and catalogue indexing.
