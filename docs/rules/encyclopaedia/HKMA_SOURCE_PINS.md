## Why this matters

The Hong Kong Mahjong Association (HKMA) publishes current first-party rules for multiple named Mahjong profiles. This is stronger provenance than generic Hong Kong/Guangdong comparison pages and gives A1 a direct authority trail.

First-party rule hub:

- https://www.hkmahjong.org/rules?lang=en

The hub separately identifies:

1. **Hong Kong Mahjong** — also described there as 清章麻雀 / Classical Mahjong;
2. **16-Tile Modern Mahjong** — 港式十六張新章麻雀;
3. Riichi Mahjong.

The first two are relevant to the regional A1 frontier.

## Copyright/provenance boundary

HKMA's rule hub states that the association owns the copyright in its rule content and permits specified forms of viewing/display/download/printing/publication subject to attribution and format/copyright conditions, with separate permission language for tournament reproduction and other uses.

For Encyclopaedia research we should therefore:

- store minimal factual index data, not copy explanatory prose or layouts;
- attribute HKMA clearly;
- keep source URLs reader-accessible;
- avoid reproducing the rulebooks wholesale.
