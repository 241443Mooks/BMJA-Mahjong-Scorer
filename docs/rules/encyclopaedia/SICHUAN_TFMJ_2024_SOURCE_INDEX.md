## Profile facts relevant to later runtime work

The standard defines a Sichuan Bloody-style competition profile including:

- 108 suited tiles; honours are absent from the standard SBR family;
- a required voided suit (`定缺`);
- Bloody continuation: play continues after a player wins until three players have won or the wall is exhausted;
- multiple winners on one discard;
- next-hand dealer based on the first winner, with the discarder becoming dealer when there are multiple simultaneous first winners;
- explicit kong payments, call-transfer / refund treatment, ready-hand checking and wall-exhaustion settlement.

These are profile/table facts, not additional Encyclopaedia count entries unless separately represented by a future concept model.

The source groups them as:

- four basic hand forms;
- three kong categories;
- six additional fan factors;
- self-draw base addition.

The source also states that scoring is capped at three fan and that unrelated categories may combine subject to the stated scoring principles.

## Relationship to MIL SBR

MIL independently publishes a short first-party **Brief Introduction to Bloody Mahjong (Sichuan Bloody Rules)**. Its finite Fan table contains nine named fan concepts: Root, All Pungs, Golden Single Wait, Full Flush, Seven Pairs, Win after Kong, Shoot after Kong, Robbing the Kong and Under the Sea.

That brief is useful corroboration and later profile-delta evidence, but it is **not counted as another 9-entry A1 corpus here**. It is a different presentation/profile summary in the same broad SBR family and would inflate the inventory without adding a separately justified complete catalogue.

If a current versioned MIL SBR competition rulebook is later recovered directly, compare it to T/TFMJ 01-2024 as a distinct named profile/version before deciding whether it merits a separate counted corpus.
