## Safe relationship/topic opportunities

The remaining corpus supports a few useful learner families without forcing broad equivalence:

- **Wind-pair + three-suit family** — extend the already-authored Windy Ones/Nines/Chow area with Chop Suey, Chow Mein, Windvane, Three Sisters and Seven Brothers. The common learner core is all four Winds with one Wind paired plus three suited groups; the rank/Chow restriction differs by treatment.
- **1–9 run + honour family** — Guardian Winds, Guardian Dragons and Dragon's Tail can sit beside the already-authored Run, Pung & Pair / Grand Sequence material, while remaining distinct treatments.
- **Greta's 1–7 run family** — Greta's Dragon and Greta's Garden share a one-suit 1–7 loose run with different honour structures.
- **Sunrise / Sunset pair** — mirrored three-suit non-terminal Pung/Kong hands with different Wind/Dragon anchors.
- **Dragon + suit/colour topic** — Blue Mountains, White Elephant, Driven Snow, Dragon's Scales, Lily of the Valley, Red Waratah, Royal Ruby, Red Lily, Ruby Jade and Lillypilly can be linked to existing Green Jade / Red Coral / White Opal through a browse topic, without claiming the variants are equivalent.
- **Mixed Chow family** — Three Philosophers, Crazy Chows, Apple Blossom and The Professors share the source/runtime Mixed Chow structure but retain distinct additional requirements.
- **Up / Down You Go pair** — mirrored fixed Wind-plus-suit multiplicity layouts.
- **Western Gates-named topic** — Confused Gates, Golden Gates and Dragon's Gates should be cross-linked for disambiguation, explicitly *not* merged with Gates of Heaven merely because of the word `Gates`.

Everything else may remain a fully useful standalone verified treatment if no stronger relationship adds learner value.
