## Inventory boundary rules established

Count useful, finite **source-local scoring concepts**. Do not inflate A1 with:

- primitive arithmetic/base-score rows;
- settlement-only transactions or penalty rows;
- the same inherited catalogue repeated under a derived profile;
- architecture-only hypothetical profiles;
- incomplete or non-gap-checkable tables;
- generic regional syntheses presented as if they were one authoritative ruleset;
- proprietary/paid catalogues reconstructed without appropriate access;
- silently repaired source ambiguities.

Authority grades remain explicit: first-party maintained ruleset, formal standard, association source, organiser competition profile, first-party implementation profile, bounded published profile, secondary/curated source, recovered copy and historical edition/synthesis are not interchangeable labels.
