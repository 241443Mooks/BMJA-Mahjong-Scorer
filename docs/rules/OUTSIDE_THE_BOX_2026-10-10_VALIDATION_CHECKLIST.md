# Outside the Box — 10 October 2026 table validation

This is a practical club-session checklist, not a formal research study.

## Before play

- Confirm that **Outside the Box** rules are active.
- Check that the displayed rules name, version and provenance make sense to players.
- Start a normal game and agree that any unusual event will be recorded as table evidence, not improvised into the app.

## During normal play

- Score one ordinary hand and one known special hand.
- Notice whether values and exposed-hand treatment feel familiar.
- Ask whether players understand settlement without a developer explanation.

## High-value checks

- Three Great Scholars value.
- One OTB Western hand, ideally Sparrow's Sanctuary.
- One local exposure override if it arises naturally (Green Jade, Red Coral or White Opal).
- Buried Treasure plus a Flower/Season, if practical.

## After a draw

- Confirm that moving into Goulash feels right.
- Check blank entry and the no-Chow message are understandable.
- If it naturally occurs, observe a repeated draw and then a winner transition.

## Incidents

Do not manufacture disruptions during normal club play. If Cannon, No choice!, or false Mah Jong arises naturally, check the recorded flow. Otherwise show Rachel the incident screen briefly after play and ask whether the consequences match club practice.

## Open source questions for Rachel

1. For an ordinary false discard name/pickup, who receives the documented 50?
2. How exactly does the club interpret the `only possible tile` 2-point bonus?
3. What is the exact first-wall-draw limit condition and terminology?
4. If not already confirmed in repository evidence, may Outside the Box be named publicly as a Mahjong Reference rules profile?

## Observe

- Can players use it without the developer narrating every step?
- Which wording causes hesitation?
- What do players naturally call the rules?
- Which table question sends them looking for help?
- Does the app reduce arithmetic or table overhead?

## After play

- Record feedback as source/provenance evidence.
- Create issues for confirmed corrections.
- Do not silently change rules from casual verbal comments.
