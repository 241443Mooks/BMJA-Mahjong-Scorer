## Baseline and scope

At the requested baseline, `westernTmSpecialHandBindings` contains **85 bindings**. The current exact-profile truth index has **one** treatment, `thirteen-unique-wonders` / Unique Wonder. Big Robert is two separate bindings with mutually exclusive structures and is assessed as two candidate rows.

## Evidence hierarchy and method

1. `docs/rules/TM_COMPANION_CATALOGUE_INDEX.md` records checked 1997 Companion photographs, named structures, synopsis/detail locators and reviewed implementation notes. It is primary evidence only for the supplementary hand facts it shows, not ordinary Western play.
2. Atlas v0.2 batches 1–4 validations and the current manifest prove learner/reference identity coverage only; they do not authorize a source proposition or cross-profile equivalence.
3. `docs/rules/SOURCE_REGISTER.md` pins `tm-companion` as a partial primary source for names, structures, values and indexes. `tm-game-illustrated` remains unchecked for ordinary play. `SPECIAL_HANDS_PROVENANCE.md` is a conservative semantic crosswalk, not a source.
4. The exact runtime binding and detector identify the behavior to compare; runtime behavior is never evidence about the book. No BMJA, OTB or Buzzard evidence is used to establish Western claims.
5. Semantic identity is conservative. The rows propose `pattern.western-tm.<binding-id>` unless a reviewed Western proposition independently proves a shared subject.

`source-certified` means the Companion source fact is recorded in the reviewed index. `source-certified` means a candidate fact and locator are recorded in the reviewed Companion evidence index; this supports special-hand truth even while ordinary Western rules remain provisional. Runtime/source equivalence is assessed only against the concise proposition in the index, not inferred from tests or binding descriptions.
