# 1. Core positioning

## Primary product description

British Mahjong Scorer is a browser-based companion for scoring and learning British Mahjong.

It helps players calculate individual hands, score complete four-player games, understand why a score applies, track settlement and running balances, and save a clear game record — without requiring an account.

The scorer is designed to reduce the amount of scoring knowledge a player has to keep in their head while keeping the reasoning visible.

## Core promise

> **Score British Mahjong without keeping the whole rulebook in your head.**

## Supporting promise

> **Tell the scorer what you know. It works out what it safely can.**

## Trust promise

> **Unknown means unknown. The scorer does not invent missing evidence.**

## Product model in one line

> **Context → Evidence → Score → Explain → Settle → Record**

## Product principle

> **Explain the game. Do not make the player learn the scoring engine.**

# 20. Trust copy bank

## Independent-project wording

British Mahjong Scorer is an independent project and is not an official BMJA application.

## Rules-scope wording

The scorer implements the British Mahjong rules documented by this project. Different clubs and groups may use house rules or interpretations that produce a different result.

## Accuracy wording

The scorer is designed to apply supported rules from the evidence entered. Where required evidence is unknown, it should withhold the unsupported rule rather than invent an answer.

## Disagreement wording

If the scorer and your table disagree, check the scoring breakdown and relevant rule. The difference may come from entered evidence, a house rule, a project interpretation or an implementation issue.

## Manual-score wording

A manually entered score is accepted for game progression and settlement, but it is not presented as a tile-verified hand.

## Partial-hand wording

A partial losing hand can score directly evidenced components, but it is not presented as a complete reconstruction and whole-hand deductions wait for complete evidence.

## Local-storage wording

In-progress game recovery is stored locally in the browser. It is not account-based cloud backup or cross-device sync.

# 32. “Why this exists” copy bank

## Personal/problem framing

British Mahjong is enjoyable enough without stopping after every hand to remember which points double, whether a pattern qualifies and how much everyone now owes everyone else.

The scorer exists to move that work out of the players' heads without turning the game into a black box.

## Product framing

The goal is not to automate away the game. It is to remove the scoring friction around it.

## Trust framing

A scoring tool is only useful if you can understand why it reached the answer. That is why explanations, evidence and the confirmed game ledger are treated as part of the core product rather than optional extras.
