## 2026-09-15 — Evidence-led Mahjong Table Companion discovery

**Objective:** Mine public user-generated evidence for repeated table jobs, pain, workarounds, contrary evidence and commercial signals without validating a predetermined feature list.

### Research passes

1. Repository protocol and schema review.
2. Scoring friction and table flow.
3. Ruleset identity, inherited rules and house rules.
4. Contextual rule questions and disputes.
5. Teaching and onboarding.
6. Table state, settlement and administration.
7. Input friction: touch, camera and voice.
8. Existing products and willingness-to-pay.
9. Contrary/null searches.

### Coverage

- Retained synthesis observations: 101
- Unique original source URLs: 50
- Supporting: 72
- Contrary: 17
- Neutral: 12
- Known bias: Riichi over-represented; modern BMJA, Singapore/Malaysian and some regional traditions under-sampled.
- Access restriction: no private Facebook groups, Discord servers or inaccessible communities were claimed as evidence.

### Main jobs reinforced

- Score a physical hand without breaking flow.
- Resolve a rule/scoring question in context.
- Teach a group without overwhelming them.
- Keep table state and settlement organised.
- Understand, agree, preserve and share how a table plays.
- Get physical state into software with minimum friction.

### Contrary / weakening evidence

- Experienced players can become faster than an app.
- Simple printed references or a designated scorer are often adequate substitutes.
- Some tables simplify or omit scoring.
- My Table persistence and willingness to pay were not established.
- Camera is already competitively served and has correction/context overhead.
- Comparable first-person demand for voice entry was not found.
