# OPP-006 — Progressive teaching / newcomer mode

**Status:** promising

## Jobs addressed

JTBD-005, JTBD-003.

## Opportunity

Reveal only the next useful concept or explanation during play so newcomers can participate without absorbing an entire ruleset up front.

## Candidate shapes

Just-in-time prompts, beginner table card, tile-literacy support and explain-why during scoring.

## Free / Plus hypothesis

Core beginner help/reference likely belongs in Free. Personalised progression is a Plus candidate only if users continue to value it across sessions.

## Risk

Teaching demand can collapse quickly as players gain experience; a good printed aid may be enough.

## Cheapest test

Observe first 3–5 sessions with and without progressive prompts; measure host interventions and independent play.
