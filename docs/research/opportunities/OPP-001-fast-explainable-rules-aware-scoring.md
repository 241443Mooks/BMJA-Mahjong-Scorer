# OPP-001 — Fast, explainable rules-aware scoring

**Status:** promising

## Jobs addressed

JTBD-002, JTBD-003, JTBD-006.

## Opportunity

Deliver a correct score and settlement quickly under the active rules profile, with an auditable explanation of why the result applies.

## Why it matters

Scoring interruption is the strongest recurring job, but free/manual substitutes are strong. Differentiation must be speed + trust + rules context, not merely another calculator.

## Free / Plus hypothesis

**Free:** correct low-friction scoring and basic explanation.  
**Plus:** only validated convenience/continuity/context layers; do not paywall correctness.

## Key risk / kill criterion

If the complete interaction is slower or more disruptive than the table's existing method, this opportunity fails regardless of calculation accuracy.

## Cheapest test

Benchmark hand-end -> accepted score against current manual/reference/app methods on real tables.
