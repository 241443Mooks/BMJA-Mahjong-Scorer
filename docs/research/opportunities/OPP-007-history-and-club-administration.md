# OPP-007 — History and club administration

**Status:** explore

## Jobs addressed

JTBD-008, JTBD-009.

## Opportunity

Retain games/results and support organiser workflows only where repeated continuity or administration pain is demonstrated, without turning the consumer Table Companion into tournament software prematurely.

## Candidate shapes

Simple game history/resume, exportable results and later optional organiser tools.

## Free / Plus hypothesis

Simple local history may be cheap Free value. Dedicated club workflows should only become paid scope after direct organiser willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Key risks

Weak leading-pain evidence, specialist audience, large scope expansion.

## Cheapest test

Observe/interview club organisers and run a manual concierge workflow before building.
