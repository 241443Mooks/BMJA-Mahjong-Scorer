# JTBD-002 — Score a physical hand without breaking flow

**Status:** validated synthesis  
**Evidence strength:** 5/5  
**Evidence status:** recovered 2026-09-15 synthesis; reopen original sources for decision-critical observations.

## Job statement

When a physical hand ends, I want to determine the correct score and settlement quickly, so that play continues without a long arithmetic or lookup pause.

## Observed friction

Arithmetic/lookup burden, social latency while one person calculates, and software entry that can itself become slower than manual scoring.

## Workarounds

Printed tables, calculators, spreadsheets, designated scorer, simplified/no scoring.

## Important contrary evidence

Experienced players can become faster than an app and some use only a compact reference card.

## Candidate opportunities

- OPP-001 Fast, explainable rules-aware scoring
- OPP-005 Camera-assisted capture

## Commercial signal

Medium but not decisive. The success criterion is end-to-end hand-end-to-accepted-score time, not mere correctness.
