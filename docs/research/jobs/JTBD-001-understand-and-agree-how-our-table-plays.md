## Job statement

When our rules have been inherited, adapted or mixed, I want to understand what we actually play and where it differs from recognised traditions, so that everyone has a shared baseline.

## Observed friction

- Unnamed or mixed traditions.
- Oral transmission and rule drift.
- Difficulty finding the right reference or software profile.

## Workarounds

Ask communities, compare rulebooks, verbally agree house rules.

## Consequences

Wrong assumptions, disagreement and difficulty configuring scoring/reference.
