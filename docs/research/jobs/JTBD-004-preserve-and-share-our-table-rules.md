## Job statement

When our group has settled on its own way of playing, I want to preserve and communicate those rules, so that we do not reconstruct or argue about them every time and newcomers can join consistently.

## Workarounds

Google Docs, custom rulebooks, handwritten sheets, club booklets and laminated cards.

## Consequences

Rule drift, repeated teaching work and loss of local/family tradition.
