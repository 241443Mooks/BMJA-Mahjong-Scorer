# JTBD-005 — Teach a group without overwhelming them

**Status:** validated synthesis  
**Evidence strength:** 5/5  
**Evidence status:** recovered 2026-09-15 synthesis.

## Job statement

When I introduce new people to Mahjong, I want to give them only the help they need at the moment they need it, so that they can play without being overwhelmed by rules and scoring detail.

## Observed friction

Too much detail at once, online play hiding physical-table work, unfamiliar tile symbols and dependence on the host.

## Workarounds

Cheat sheets, laminated aids, staged verbal teaching and labelled tiles.

## Candidate opportunities

- OPP-006 Progressive teaching/newcomer mode
- OPP-003 Contextual rule explainer

## Commercial signal

Weak as a standalone subscription job because need often decays as the group becomes competent.
