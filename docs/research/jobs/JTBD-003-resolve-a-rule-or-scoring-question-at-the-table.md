## Job statement

When an unfamiliar or disputed situation occurs, I want to know what applies under the rules we are actually playing, so that we can resolve the question quickly and confidently.

## Observed friction

Generic references do not map neatly to the exact situation; players can be unsure whether a remembered rule is standard, variant-specific or local.

## Workarounds

Ask the table expert/community, search rule sites, contact product support.
