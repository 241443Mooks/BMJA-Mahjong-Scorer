## Scoring guidance

Use 1-5 only where there is enough evidence to compare jobs.

- **Evidence strength:** isolated anecdote -> repeated across communities with visible workarounds.
- **Pain severity:** mild inconvenience -> prevents/confuses play or creates repeated conflict/delay.
- **Frequency:** rare -> repeated every hand/game/session.
- **Product fit:** peripheral -> directly aligned with a quiet fifth person / Table Companion.
- **Pay signal:** no evidence -> repeated credible willingness to pay or paid alternatives already used.

Do not calculate a mechanically weighted total. Priority should remain a reasoned product judgement that cites the underlying evidence.
