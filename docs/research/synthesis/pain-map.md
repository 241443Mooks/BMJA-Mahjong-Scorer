## Cross-cutting interpretation

The highest-value territory is recurring **interruption around a physical table**: calculation, lookup, explanation, settlement and shared state. The strongest product concept is a trusted, rules-aware quiet fifth person, not a broad operating system.

## Important counterweights

- Experienced players can become faster than an app.
- Some groups use a single card, a designated scorer or simplified/no scoring.
- House-rule documentation may be solved by a one-off sheet rather than persistent software.
- Teaching demand often decays.
- Camera input is already served and has correction/context costs.
- Voice demand was not established.
