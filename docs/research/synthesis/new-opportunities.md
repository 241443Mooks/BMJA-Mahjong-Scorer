# New opportunities emerging from evidence

## 1. Contextual “explain why” as a primary job

The stronger need is not merely rule lookup. It is understanding why a result applies to the exact hand, table state and rules profile. This can resolve disputes, teach the rule and increase trust in scoring.

## 2. Shared authoritative table state

The evidence points more strongly to one source of truth for scores, seats/winds, round state and settlement than to remembered player identities as an independent job.

## 3. Accessibility and tile-literacy support

A weaker but distinct signal appears around players who struggle with characters/symbols on physical tiles. Optional transliteration/labels or visual reminders may help newcomers and mixed-language family tables.

## 4. Auditable trust as differentiation

Existing-app complaints about scoring correctness suggest that source-linked, inspectable reasoning may itself be valuable: “this score is X because A/B/C under table profile Y”.

These remain opportunity hypotheses, not roadmap commitments.
