# Video 03 — West: Flowers, Seasons and Bonus-Tile Scoring

**Working title:** Flowers and Seasons in British Mahjong Scoring

**Series context:** same four-player hand. South has gone Mah Jong. West is a non-winner. Prevailing Wind is East.

**Target score:** **40**

## Canonical entered evidence

- player Wind: **West**
- prevailing Wind: **East**
- non-winner
- partial evidence
- exposed Pung of 1 Characters = 4 points
- concealed Pung of 5 Circles = 4 points
- exposed Pung of 4 Bamboo = 2 points
- pair of West Winds = 2 points
- Flower 3 = 4 points + 1 double because West corresponds to number 3
- Season 2 = 4 points, no double for West

Calculation:

`4 + 4 + 2 + 2 + 4 + 4 = 20 points`

`20 × 2 = 40`

## Core lesson

Flowers and Seasons are bonus tiles. They do not occupy the normal structural hand slots, but they score points and can also produce doubles when they match the player's Wind.

## Shot order and narration

### 1. Set context

Set player Wind to West, prevailing Wind to East, non-winner.

> West has not gone Mah Jong, so again we can enter only the scoring evidence we want to count. This hand is useful because it also has Flowers and Seasons.

### 2. Add exposed Pung of 1 Characters

> This is an exposed Pung of 1 Characters. Ones and nines are major tiles even though they belong to a suit, so an exposed major Pung scores 4 points.

### 3. Add concealed Pung of 5 Circles

> Next is a concealed Pung of 5 Circles. Five is a minor tile, but because this Pung is concealed it also scores 4 points.

### 4. Add exposed Pung of 4 Bamboo

> This exposed Pung of 4 Bamboo is a minor Pung, so it scores 2 points.

### 5. Add pair of West Winds

> West also has a pair of West Winds. A pair of your own Wind scores 2 points.

### 6. Add Flower 3

> Now the bonus tiles. Every Flower scores 4 points. The Winds also map to the bonus-tile numbers: East is 1, South is 2, West is 3 and North is 4.

> This is Flower 3, so for West it is the player's own Flower. It gives 4 points and one double.

### 7. Add Season 2

> Season 2 still scores its normal 4 points, but number 2 belongs to South rather than West, so it does not give West another double.

### 8. Show calculation

> That gives us 20 basic points altogether. Flower 3 gives one double, so 20 doubled once is 40.

### 9. Close

> West therefore scores 40. We now have South on 60, East on 56 and West on 40. Next we will score North, where the main feature is a Kong.
